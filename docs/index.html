In its flurry of Vietnam War-era reforms in the 1970s, Congress enacted the War Powers Resolution as a framework to manage the divided war powers that the Constitution gives to both the legislature and the commander in chief. The law was enacted over President Richard Nixon’s veto, and sought to clarify and limit the circumstances under which presidents could utilize military force without congressional acquiescence. Further, it enacted requirements to increase presidential reporting to and consultation with Congress. Post-Nixon presidents have expressed doubts about and outright opposition to various aspects of these provisions’ constitutionality.

In addition to those features, this statutory scheme reserved to Congress the power to pass resolutions overriding presidents when they deployed military force without congressional authorization. These resolutions were “privileged,” meaning any member of Congress could force a debate and vote on them.

Originally, war powers resolutions were meant to become binding upon passage through Congress. However, in 1983, the Supreme Court ruled in its landmark case INS v. Chadha that so-called “legislative vetoes” were unconstitutional unless they were presented to the president for signature. But, of course, this tool was created for Congress to oppose the president, and so any passed resolutions presented to the president would inevitably be vetoed. Thus, it appeared that unless Congress could muster the two-thirds supermajority required to overcome a presidential veto, they would amount to nothing more than press releases.

Yet members of Congress keep introducing and voting on them. IT之家 11 月 26 日消息，汽车研究平台 iSeeCars.com 近日发布研究报告，分析美国 2026 年 10 月超 110 万辆车龄 1-5 年的二手车销售数据，按照动力系统、车型类别和具体车型， 统计车辆从上市到售出的平均天数 。

该报告显示，特斯拉 Cybertruck 是美国销售速度最快的二手皮卡， 平均挂牌 39.1 天就能卖出 ，而所有皮卡的平均销售周期为 65.5 天。Cybertruck 的销售速度超过了其他所有皮卡，包括排名第二的福特 Maverick Hybrid 混动皮卡，以及排名第三的 Rivian R1T。

从平均挂牌销售天数来看，纯电动车甚至已经超过了混合动力汽车。纯电动车的平均销售周期为 45.6 天，混合动力汽车为 54.5 天，汽油车则为 61 天。

IT之家注意到，凯迪拉克 Escalade IQ 是此次调查中销售速度最慢的车型，平均挂牌销售时间达到 134.3 天，是整个汽车行业平均水平的两倍以上。

此外该平台表示，Cybertruck 在停产以前就相对稀缺，因此市场需求被推动。目前进入二手车市场的主要是租赁车，与官方建议零售价相比，二手 Cybertruck 的价格相当便宜。

English original

In an extraordinary decision, the Federal Communications Commission on Wednesday granted SpaceX conditional authorization to deploy a constellation of up to 1 million Starmind satellites designed to serve as orbital data centers.

If fully deployed, this constellation would increase the number of satellites in orbit by roughly 50-fold.

“The United States is moving decisively to ensure that this breakthrough technology creates opportunity, drives investment, and delivers lasting benefits for the American people,” FCC Chairman Brendan Carr said in a statement on Wednesday. “This action opens a bold new frontier for the computing infrastructure needed to power America’s leadership and growth.”

The decision, announced a day before the Thanksgiving holiday in the United States, represents a major regulatory victory for SpaceX and its founder, Elon Musk, who has allied himself with the Trump administration.

This announcement clears a major hurdle to deploying data centers in space, although it does not establish that orbital data centers are technically or economically viable. Nevertheless, the FCC authorization appears designed to give the company wide latitude as it works through these remaining issues.

For example, SpaceX has seven years to deploy a single operational Starmind satellite under the order. SpaceX has until November 2035 to deploy 100,000 operational satellites, and November 2040 for the full constellation. SpaceX, therefore, has a 14-year regulatory runway to deploy the most ambitious industrial project ever approved for spaceflight.

An assistant professor and information media librarian at the University of Tennessee at Martin was fired last month for using the women’s restroom, the institution’s student newspaper, The Pacer , reported .

Johnnie LaDue told the paper last week that she had been confronted by the library’s dean after someone filed a complaint about her use of a staff-only women’s restroom in the library. She later received a written warning citing the Riley Gaines Women’s Safety and Protection Act, a Tennessee state law that went into effect in July that requires people to use the restroom that corresponds with their sex assigned at birth.

But LaDue declined to use an alternative restroom, noting that the only single-occupancy restroom available was farther away than the women’s room and was located in a room where her co-workers ate and socialized, making it awkward to use.

“I decided to stay principled,” she told The Pacer . “I’m a woman. I’m going to use the women’s room, and if you’re going to fire me for that, that’s on you. But I’m not going to change who I am or diminish who I am.”
