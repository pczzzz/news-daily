美国的交易正在加强查韦斯政权

一月份美国军方抓获时任委内瑞拉总统尼古拉斯·马杜罗后，美国开始执行一项任务，修复唐纳德·特朗普总统所说的“死亡国家”。特朗普政府的官员对委内瑞拉的议程拥有广泛的权力，承诺让该国摆脱社会主义。正如国务卿马可·鲁比奥（Marco Rubio）在一月底被传唤到参议院时所说，政府的最终目标是建立“一个友好、稳定、繁荣和民主的委内瑞拉，让社会各阶层在自由和公正的选举中得到代表。”

卢比奥为管理这一民主转型制定了一个三阶段框架：稳定、经济复苏，最后是转型。华盛顿目前的重点是第二阶段，即复苏经济，但其进展速度如此之快，透明度如此之低，以至于完全危及既定的最终目标。尤其是美国与该国石油行业的交易正在具体化而不是改变委内瑞拉的经济租金主义：在这种治理模式中，国家控制经济的重要部门，并向支持其议程的公司提供商业合同和市场特权。正是这种制度助长了委内瑞拉国家在过去25年里腐败猖獗，让独裁领导人淹没了民主力量并赢得了选举。

U.S. Dealmaking Is Strengthening the Chavista Regime

Javier Corrales is Dwight W. Morrow 1895 Professor of Political Science at Amherst College and the author of Autocracy Rising: How Venezuela Transitioned to Authoritarianism .

After the U.S. military captured then Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro in January, the United States set out on a mission to fix what President Donald Trump called a “dead country.” With broad authority over Venezuela’s agenda, officials in the Trump administration promised to free the country from socialism. As Secretary of State Marco Rubio said, when he was called before the Senate in late January, the administration’s ultimate objective was to establish “a friendly, stable, prosperous Venezuela, and democratic, in which all elements of society are represented in free and fair elections.”

Rubio laid out a three-stage framework for managing this democratic transition: stabilization, economic recovery, and, finally, transition. Washington is currently focused on the second stage, recovering the economy, but it is proceeding so quickly and with such little transparency that it is jeopardizing the stated end goal entirely. U.S. dealmaking with the country’s oil sector, in particular, is reifying rather than transforming Venezuela’s economic rentism: a model of governance in which the state controls important sectors of the economy and offers business contracts and market privileges to firms that support its agenda. This is precisely the system that facilitated the Venezuelan state’s rampant corruption over the past 25 years, allowing autocratic leaders to drown out democratic forces and win elections.

As it pursues its economic goals with dizzying speed, the Trump administration is also slow-walking democratization efforts. For months, Washington excluded opposition leader María Corina Machado and her party, Vente Venezuela, from any direct involvement in negotiations. Machado had left the country last December to receive a Nobel Peace Prize after nearly 20 months in hiding, and after Maduro’s removal, the United States did little to help her return. Venezuela’s pro-democracy forces have thus been left feeling like they are pushing the regime in Caracas to democratize with the acquiescence, but not necessarily the full enthusiasm, of the United States.

Last month, Machado returned to Venezuela , and now she has the potential to change the game. Machado could become the greatest asset or, possibly, the greatest disruptor to Venezuela’s democratic transition. If Machado coordinates with the faction of the opposition that is currently negotiating with the government, any outcome will be seen as legitimate. But if she opposes and speaks out against the result, Venezuelans will find themselves in a familiar and dangerous place: with a divided opposition, a government with few constraints on its power, and a transition in purgatory.

11月20日，省统计局发布了1—10月份全省经济运行报告，报告显示，1—10月，全省上下坚持稳中求进工作总基调，扎实推进高质量发展，加力发挥宏观政策效能，新动能加快成长，企业效益持续改善，就业物价保持稳定，经济运行向稳向好基础进一步夯实。

1—10月份，全省规模以上工业增加值同比下降7.0%。从三大门类看，采矿业增加值同比下降9.2%，制造业下降2.7%，电力、热力、燃气及水生产和供应业下降4.4%。从行业看，煤层气采掘业、有色金属、建材工业分别增长14.7%、2.8%、1.1%。从产品看，非常规天然气、精甲醇、白酒、化学药品原药产品产量分别增长10.0%、9.7%、7.8%、4.3%。从效益看，前三季度，全省规模以上工业企业实现营业收入21721.7亿元，增长1.9%；实现利润总额1136.1亿元，增长36.4%。

工业新动能加快成长。1—10月份，全省规上计算机及办公设备制造业增加值同比增长61.3%，通用设备制造业增长13.3%，新材料产业增长7.9%，汽车制造业增长3.7%。工业新产品中，集成电路、服务机器人、工业控制计算机及系统、新能源汽车产量分别增长10.9倍、4.1倍、1.2倍、90.9%。

1—10月份，全省固定资产投资同比下降15.3%。分产业看，第一产业投资同比下降19.9%，第二产业投资下降8.3%，第三产业投资下降20.2%。分领域看，房地产开发投资下降25.6%，基础设施投资下降5.6%。传统优势行业投资稳步增长，非金属矿采选业投资增长79.9%，黑色金属冶炼和压延加工业增长59.6%，化学纤维制造业增长49.9%。

1—10月份，全省新建商品房销售面积1321.6万平方米，同比下降11.8%，降幅较前三季度收窄0.2个百分点；新建商品房销售额911.8亿元，下降12.8%，降幅收窄0.3个百分点。

1—10月份，全省社会消费品零售总额6443.1亿元，同比下降3.7%，其中限额以上消费品零售额2493.6亿元，下降11.2%。分城乡看，限额以上城镇消费品零售额2276.5亿元，同比下降11.9%；乡村消费品零售额217.1亿元，下降4.2%。分消费形态看，商品零售2275.2亿元，同比下降12.2%；餐饮收入218.4亿元，下降0.1%。基本生活类消费稳定增长。1—10月份，全省限额以上日用品类零售额同比增长3.5%，其中可穿戴智能设备零售额增长1.2倍；中西药品类零售额增长3.2%。
